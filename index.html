The model selector compares an unguarded context arm with a tail-safe arm. Both results are same-date research on four videos; neither has formal CurrentCourt admission, production Rally, KeepTimeline acceptance, or export.

Editorial labels are not physical truth. A separate two-date replication did not establish generalization. No per-video rows, exact timelines, source identifiers, or source assets are included in this page.
