Offline LIVE/DEAD and Rally interval study

The 2026-10-12 report records a real improvement in offline editorial-state and boundary modeling. It does not meet production automatic-editing acceptance. This page summarizes the headline comparison in English; the complete technical report is preserved in its original Chinese.

Study scope

The comparison covers four complete videos recorded on 2026-10-03. The reference contains 148 human-edited editorial intervals. Editorial labels describe editing relevance; they are not physical shuttle-in-play or Hit truth. The two arms were developed on this single-date cohort, so the result does not establish cross-date generalization.

How to read the result

Tail-safe matched 79 of 148 editorial intervals, three fewer than the unguarded arm. It lost 164.33 seconds of editorial LIVE and kept 62.85 seconds of editorial DEAD. The unguarded context arm matched 82 of 148, lost 175.60 seconds of editorial LIVE, and kept 51.12 seconds of editorial DEAD.

The Tail-safe arm therefore reduced LIVE loss by 11.27 seconds while retaining 11.73 more seconds of DEAD. The 87.75% balanced accuracy is a state-classification score, not a Rally detection accuracy. Interval matching is a separate task and has a separate denominator.

What remains unproven

  • The Court, temporal-stability, and event-ownership contracts are not satisfied for formal production consumption.
  • The research comparison produced zero accepted production Rally intervals, zero KeepTimeline outputs, and zero new exports.
  • The evidence does not establish physical LIVE/DEAD truth, verified shuttle trajectories, or new-date generalization.
  • EventFirst remains the implemented production path with status PRODUCTION_NOT_READY.
